The reading-time calculation is duplicated in `PostController::index` and `PostController::show`. Extract it into a class `App\Support\ReadingTime` with a static method `for(string $markdown): string` that returns the label (e.g. "3 min read"), and use it in both places. Also, numbers like "13" or "2026" are currently not counted as words, so posts full of figures look shorter than they are: count them too.
